Employee attrition at Canterra Machine Learning Foundations and Advanced ML & AI, team project

Why do people leave Canterra, and can HR see it coming?

Duplicated HR records made our models look 99% accurate when they caught only 13% of leavers on new employees. Once fixed, the real drivers were a poor work environment and frequent travel, not pay: four actions worth about $3.9M a year. A tuned Random Forest then put 41% of leavers in the riskiest 20% of staff.

Pay equity at BSR Using Data to Lead Change and Visualization & Storytelling, team project

Median pay says equal. Mean pay says $15,446. Which one is lying?

Neither. Pay is equal for four in five employees, and 85 Directors make up 95% of the gap, which held at 27.1% across 30 robustness models. We took it to leadership as a memo and deck, then as a five-dashboard Tableau story. I built and presented the Junior vs. Director dashboard.

Airbnb pricing in DC Stats for Business Analytics and Programming I, team project

What actually moves the nightly price of a DC Airbnb?

Size, then location. Each extra bedroom is worth about $48 a night holding location fixed, and Downtown or Georgetown adds $60 to $90. Superhost status and ratings add nothing once size and location are in the model.
